# Source categories and minimum viable evidence

For Nature and Biodiversity Investment Agent, obtain the actual issuer/project financials, instrument or programme contracts, relevant outcome reports, point-in-time market data and applicable methods. The host must independently discover, retrieve and review these sources through authorized tools. The Python runtime performs no automatic network calls.

Prefer issuer and debt-management-office documents, signed offering/financing terms, official statistical and development-finance sources, primary methodology providers and independent outcome-verification reports. A vendor ESG label, news story or investment-manager opinion may be contextual evidence, not a substitute for the controlling contract or outcome measurement. Record licences and redistribution limits for every dataset. Optional commercial market/ESG/nature data is neither connected nor redistributed.

For bond quotes use a market source that identifies instrument, timestamp, price convention and quote status. For ecological data record spatial precision, coverage, methodology/version and validation. For sovereign debt select the appropriate country DSA and reconcile debt scope, currency and fiscal periods. Do not infer source availability merely from a connector name or a URL.
