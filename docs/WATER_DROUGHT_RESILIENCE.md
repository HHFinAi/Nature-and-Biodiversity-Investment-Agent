# Water, drought and nature-resilience diligence overlay

**18 November 2026 | Original HHFinAI methodology extension. Research-only, not water-rights/legal advice.**

Physical climate adaptation interacts with nature risk but is **not the same** as biodiversity improvement. A farm or data center may become more commercially resilient while stressing water resources and downstream communities. Conversely, a healthy watershed may support resilience without creating a tradable claim or observable company return.

## Required place-based facts
- Geographic **asset** or procurement catchment, uncertainty radius and watershed; not headquarters-only.
- Raw water sources, seasonality, groundwater/surface intake, drought hazard dataset vintage and scenario temporal scale.
- Water quantity, quality, permitted withdrawal/discharge, ecological minimum flows, stakeholder rights and rival demand; do not disclose vulnerable sensitive habitats.
- Operational dependencies, yield or process substitution, treatment requirements, storage and supply redundancy.
- Adaptation options, capex/O&M, financing authority, responsible party and remaining residual risk.
- Baseline and counterfactual site outcome (yield, avoided downtime, pollutant load or ecosystem condition), monitoring plan and independent comparison.

## Three separate ledgers
| Ledger | Example | Prohibited inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business resilience | Avoided production loss under site drought scenario | No inferred issuer EPS without segment exposure, annual event frequency and cost bridge |
| Solutions revenue | Paid water-treatment/irrigation systems from approved budget | No revenue estimate from societal water-infrastructure spending need |
| Nature / community outcomes | Ecological flow or verified water-quality improvement | No biodiversity-credit or positive impact claim from project existence |

## Suggested original study cases
1. **Irrigation and crop adaptation:** analyze a region-specific basin, agricultural producer and funded technology supplier only after legal abstraction rights, crop calendar and project cash costs are supplied. Monitor yield *and* downstream flow; beware rebound.
2. **Water-scarce digital infrastructure:** assess data-center cooling options, site-level water and power availability, permits, outages, incremental capex and capacity valuation. Do not assume volumetric water savings generate a positive equity NPV.
3. **Urban flood attenuation:** separate local drainage and water-treatment project tenders from habitat outcomes and benefit claims. Funding, service fee and maintenance must be documented.

## Research controls
Store source ID, licence, as-of date, spatial resolution, observed-versus-modeled distinction, geography exposure and original units. Do not convert NOAA El Niño **strength** probability to local rainfall/soil moisture probability; cite the [NOAA CPC advisory](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml) by vintage. A stronger ENSO forecast cannot independently predict commodity earnings.
