# AI infrastructure: water and land evidence before financial attribution

**Prepared 16 October 2026 | Primary-source research handoff | Site assessment: NEEDS_DATA.**

The [financial case and phase tracker](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/tree/main/examples/ai-infrastructure) connect deployment timing to an illustrative project model. This handoff identifies the location-level evidence needed before water or land affects that model. It assigns no biodiversity score, monetary nature value or verified outcome.

## Starting evidence

Microsoft's September 2025 Wisconsin design disclosure describes a mixed cooling approach. Its October 2025 Caledonia update says it would not proceed with the originally proposed site. These are dated company statements, not measured water performance, a regulator's decision or evidence of ecological improvement. See source IDs **MS-WI-DESIGN-20250918** and **MS-CALEDONIA-20251010** in the [primary register](https://github.com/HHFinAi/Sustainability-to-Financial-Materiality-Analysis/blob/main/examples/ai-infrastructure/evidence/sources.json).

The six tracker records include two Mount Pleasant phases; their verified capacity and permit fields are unknown. Do not aggregate records as unique campuses or infer land area, water demand or local dependency from an assumed power capacity.

## Location-level evidence gates

| Evidence gate | Required boundary | Possible financial use after verification |
|---|---|---|
| Site and phase identity | Parcel, operator, phase, utility and water-service boundary; reconcile overlapping project names | Match the right permit and expenditure to the asset |
| Water balance | Withdrawal versus consumption, direct cooling versus total site use, source, season and measurement period | Quantify supported operating costs and design trade-offs |
| Availability and restrictions | Service rights, drought triggers, competing uses and applicable authority conditions | Model an evidenced restriction or conditional scenario; basin stress alone is not outage frequency |
| Land and habitats | Disturbance footprint, baseline condition, relevant habitats and permission conditions | Identify supported mitigation, redesign or delay costs |
| Mitigation and restoration | Additionality, baseline, implementation, monitoring and affected stakeholder rights | Recognize verified costs or outcomes without netting unrelated benefits |
| Counterfactual | Same workload and location, alternative design, energy trade-off and off-site dependencies | Avoid counting water savings, avoided losses and extra capacity more than once |

These are proposed research questions. No spatial overlay, local ecological assessment, measured water balance or stakeholder consultation has been completed in this handoff. A closed-loop cooling statement does not establish zero total water use; reduced withdrawal does not by itself establish improved basin condition.

## Financial and stewardship handoff

Keep physical quantities and financial inputs separate until the causal link is supported. A restriction might affect operating availability; a redesigned cooling system might affect capex, power and maintenance; a land condition might affect schedule. An environmental score does not set a discount rate.
