# Brazil: production growth is not a yield forecast

Original calculations from CONAB first-survey forecasts dated 15 October 2026. All changes below are approximate because the source growth rates and levels are rounded.

| Crop | Forecast output (Mt) | Crop area (m ha) | Output growth | Area growth | Implied yield growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| grains | 367.1 | 85.4 | 1.4% | 2.3% | -0.88% |
| soy | 181.6 | 49.3 | 0.7% | 1.4% | -0.69% |
| corn | 148.0 | 23.7 | 2.4% | 4.9% | -2.38% |

Crop area sums planted/harvested cycles. It is not unique physical land, forest conversion or measured habitat loss. Total-grain yield also reflects crop mix.

| Illustrative price change | Revenue per hectare index | Operating profit index | Profit change |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0% | 110.00 | 35.00 | 16.67% |
| -5.0% | 104.50 | 29.50 | -1.67% |
| -10.0% | 99.00 | 24.00 | -20.00% |

Under the labelled 10% yield-improvement scenario, a -4.55% price change is the threshold for unchanged operating profit after the assumed extra cost.

At fixed output, the arithmetic crop-area requirement falls 9.09%. This is not a prediction of actual land sparing: prices, demand, expansion incentives and location evidence are missing.
